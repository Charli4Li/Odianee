Experiential dining inspired by the ancient 56 Bhoga traditions of the Puri Jagannath temple hearth. Steamed in unglazed terracotta pots over wood fires with pure desi ghee, tender vegetables, and sacred lentils.

Signature catches from Chilika Lake and the Bay of Bengal coast: delicate Rohu in zesty mustard gravy (Machha Besara), rich lake crab curry (Kankada Jhola), and tender jumbo prawns simmered with coconut milk.
